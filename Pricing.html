Pricing FAQ

Read this before you ask sales.

Is the free tier really free? What's the catch?

Really free, forever. The catch: you're capped at one course, 30 lessons, and 10 learners. That's enough to ship a real onboarding for a small team. Past that, you'll want Studio.

What happens if I exceed my learner limit on Studio?

Each learner past 100 is €1/month, billed at the end of the cycle. We'll never block a learner from logging in mid-course because of a limit — we trust you to true up later.

Do you offer non-profit or education discounts?

Yes — 50% off Studio for registered non-profits and accredited educational institutions. Email edu@neuropetitorius.com with proof and we'll set it up.

Can I cancel any time?

Monthly plans cancel at the end of the cycle. Annual plans are non-refundable past the 14-day trial. We don't auto-renew without warning — you'll get an email 30 days before your renewal.

What about VAT?

Prices are exclusive of VAT. EU customers with a valid VAT number aren't charged VAT (reverse charge). For everyone else, VAT is added at your local rate.
