📜 The case

Most teams don't have bad managers. They have bad documentation tools.

Every business already knows how to train its people. The knowledge lives in your best manager's head, in a 4-year-old PDF, in the way your veterans handle weird customers. The problem isn't knowing — it's getting that out and into a learner's hands.

The manual nobody writes is the manual everyone needs.

You've been meaning to write the SOP for two years. So has every other operator. Meanwhile your best people leave, your new hires improvise, and you find out the hard way which mistakes were preventable.

Documentation is the single highest-leverage thing a small team can do — and the single thing nobody has time for. Because writing it from scratch is miserable, and the second it's written, it's already half wrong.

"We didn't build an AI tutor. We built the tool that turns 'we should write that down' into 'it's already written, structured, and being learned.'" — from the founding notes, summer 2025
The shift

Two ways to onboard. Pick one.

The slow way

  • Block off two weeks to write a real handbook.
  • Realize it's already outdated. Rewrite chapter 3.
  • Ask your senior people to "review it." They don't.
  • Buy an LMS. Pay per seat. Watch nobody log in.
  • Sit your new hire next to someone for a week and pray.
  • Find out in month two what they were never told.

The Neuropetitorius way

  • Drop in whatever exists — even if it's barely anything.
  • Record a 20-minute voice note from your best manager.
  • Get a structured course back the same afternoon.
  • Answer the 5 gap questions to make it sharper.
  • Send a link. The new hire learns at their pace.
  • Watch where they get stuck. Fix the lesson, not the person.

What we believe

Six principles we won't compromise on.

01

Sparse content is fine.

If we can't build a real course from what you have, we'll tell you exactly what's missing — not refuse to start.

02

Voice is data.

20 minutes of audio from your best manager beats 50 pages of doc nobody wrote. We treat it as a first-class source.

03

Generated, not faked.

Every lesson is grounded in your sources. We don't invent policies you never wrote. If a fact came from your audio, we cite the timestamp.

04

Editable, always.

Generated drafts are starting points. Every lesson, quiz, and answer is yours to rewrite — and edits stick across regenerations.

05

Your data is your data.

EU-only processing. Never used for training models. Delete a course and we send you the audit log to confirm.

06

Built for buying, not begging.

Honest pricing, real free tier, transparent limits. No "contact sales" until you actually need a custom contract.
